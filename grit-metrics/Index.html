⚾ BAT SWING — Film it like this or it won't read

1) Side view, not behind. Camera on the side she swings toward, full body + full bat in frame the whole swing.
2) Bright light, plain background. No one walking behind, no fence pattern behind her — the app tracks motion, so background motion confuses it.
3) Stand still 1 sec, then swing. No practice swings in the clip.
4) Trim to 3-4 sec total. Just stand → swing → hold finish.
5) 120fps, Most Compatible (MP4). 240fps MOV is what hung last time.
6) Expect an ESTIMATE first. Swing from video is an estimate until we check it against 2-3 radar / HitTrax swings. Vertical is exact — Swing is estimate.

Do this order: Vertical first (proves it works) → Broad → Swing → 20-Yd. Don't start with Swing.

How each test is auto-measured

Vertical: Head rise (px) ÷ (Person height px ÷ Athlete height) = inches.
Broad: Feet start → feet land distance (px) ÷ same scale = inches / feet.
Swing: Fastest bat-tip motion across frames. Bat tip speed ≈ distance moved between frames × fps, scaled by athlete height. Shows mph. Best in bright light, side view, full swing in frame.
20-Yd: Motion start (first big move) → motion crosses finish side of frame. Time × 20 yards = time. For best accuracy film from side, start line left, finish line right, whole 20 yd in frame. If camera is too close, time will be LOW confidence — re-film wider.

Video won't finish uploading? Do this (30 sec fix)

1) Trim it in Photos — open video > Edit > drag ends so it's only ~5 seconds (1 sec still + jump + 1 sec after). Tap Done > Save as New Clip.
2) Export as MP4, not MOV — Share > Options > Format: Most Compatible. Or iPhone Settings > Camera > Formats > Most Compatible for next time.
3) Record 120fps, not 240fps for now — file is half the size, still accurate enough. 240fps = huge file.
4) Use Safari on iPhone — Chrome on iPhone struggles with HEVC .MOV. Safari plays it best.
5) Good WiFi, keep tab open — don't lock phone while it says Analyzing. It scans on your phone, not uploading to a server, but it still needs 10-20 sec for a 5-sec clip.

If it still hangs: film a 3-sec test clip, upload that. If the 3-sec works, your original was just too big.
